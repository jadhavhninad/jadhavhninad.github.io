My platform experience includes custom quadcopters, Pixhawk flight controllers, PX4 and ArduPilot, MAVSDK, motion-capture systems, and peripherals used in autonomous field deployment. I have integrated LiDAR, RGB-D cameras, visual-inertial odometry, acoustic sensors, software-defined radios, and drone peripherals over UART and I2C.

This systems work supports autonomous deployment outside controlled laboratory settings, including long-duration experiments at sea.
