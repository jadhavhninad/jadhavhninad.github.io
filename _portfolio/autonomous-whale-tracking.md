I design and deploy autonomous robotic systems for tracking and observing sperm whales at sea. This work combines acoustic, visual, and VHF sensing with autonomous planning on aerial and marine platforms.

The project has included waterproof, battery-powered drone payloads; real-time onboard VHF directionality estimation; autonomous quadcopters; and sensing deployments from research vessels in the Caribbean. I have participated in four approximately three-week science expeditions in Dominica and co-led two of them in coordination with Project CETI's marine operations team.
