I develop algorithms for distributed robot teams exploring unknown environments under severe communication-bandwidth constraints. The work combines heterogeneous sensing modalities—including LiDAR, ultra-wideband, and WiFi—with decentralized planning and implicit information exchange.

Related systems have been deployed on TurtleBot, ROSbot, LoCoBot, iRobot Create, Leo Rover, and ArduRover platforms using ROS-based SLAM and exploration software.
